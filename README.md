# Assignment 3: Synthetic Reading of Premchand's कफ़न

## Project Overview

This project explores Munshi Premchand's short story *कफ़न* through a Synthetic Reading approach. It combines partial reading, researcher reflection, AI-assisted thematic extraction and literary interpretation.

The study focuses on the relationship between poverty, hunger, social marginalisation and human sensitivity, particularly through the neglect of Budhiya.

## Research Question

How does Premchand's *कफ़न* explore the relationship between poverty, hunger, social marginalisation and human sensitivity, particularly through the neglect of Budhiya?

## Methodology

1. Divided the complete story into three approximately equal parts.
2. Selected beginning, middle, ending and random sentence-units from each part for partial reading.
3. Developed an initial reflection, research problem and researcher positionality.
4. Prepared a detailed prompt for AI-assisted thematic extraction.
5. Extracted themes and textual evidence using an LLM.
6. Converted the extracted analysis into a Python file.
7. Created a bar chart showing the number of extracted evidence excerpts across themes.
8. Interpreted the findings using Cultural Materialism and Intersectionality.

## Theoretical Frameworks

- **Cultural Materialism:** Examines poverty, hunger, labour and material conditions.
- **Intersectionality:** Examines the overlapping effects of caste, class and gender.

## Project Files

- `kafan_corpus.txt` – Complete story text.
- `part_1.txt`, `part_2.txt`, `part_3.txt` – Three sections of the story.
- `partial_reading.txt` – Selected sentence-units for initial reading.
- `initial_reflection.txt` – Researcher reflection and positionality.
- `analysis_prompt.txt` – Prompt used for AI-assisted extraction.
- `kafan_complete_literary_analysis.json` – Extracted thematic analysis.
- `Theme_JSON.py` – Python file containing the extracted analysis.
- `theme_visualization.png` – Visualization of extracted evidence counts.
- `findings_and_interpretation.md` – Findings and theoretical interpretation.

## Limitations

AI-generated themes and interpretations require human verification. The evidence counts in the visualization represent extracted textual excerpts, not actual theme frequency or importance. Budhiya's limited narrative voice also restricts the interpretation of her personal perspective.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Digital Humanities methods can combine computational assistance with critical literary reading. It also highlights the importance of human judgement, textual context and reflexivity when using AI for literary analysis.
